Maps & Plans

A century of plans and photographs, from the first survey to the cemetery today.

Select any map to see it full size. They were prepared in 2019 for the cemetery’s landscape guide, and the oldest drawings are the Olmsted Brothers’ own.

Credits. Plans and drawings from the St. John’s Church Memorial Cemetery Historic Landscape Management Guide (April 2019) by Heritage Landscapes LLC for the St. John’s Memorial Cemetery Committee. Historic plans courtesy of the Olmsted Archives, Frederick Law Olmsted National Historic Site.

A historic Episcopal cemetery in Laurel Hollow, New York, founded in 1862 and shaped by the Olmsted Brothers.
